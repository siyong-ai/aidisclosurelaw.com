How these pages are built

01

Enrolled text first

Requirements and penalties are taken from the bill as passed, not from a summary article. Every page links the official source.

02

Dates are a chain, not a date

Signed, effective, phased, delayed, repealed. Colorado's effective date moved twice before the law was replaced outright.

03

Enforceability is stated

If a court has barred a law, the page says so. AB 2839 and AB 2655 are not compliance obligations; they are litigation history.

04

Verification date on every page

Every page carries the date it was last checked against the primary source, so you can tell how stale it is.
